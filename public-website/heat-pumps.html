Air-to-air or air-to-water?

Both move heat using electricity, but they serve buildings differently. The correct answer depends on whether you need domestic hot water, how the building is zoned, and how much of the existing heating distribution you intend to retain.

Air-to-air

Fast zoned heating plus cooling, delivered directly to occupied spaces.

Air-to-water

Space heating and hot water through a wet system, subject to building suitability.

Hybrid thinking

Some buildings benefit from different solutions in different zones or phases.
