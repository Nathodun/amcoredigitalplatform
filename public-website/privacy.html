PRIVACY + COOKIES

Clear choices before non-essential tracking.

This prototype separates necessary site functions from analytics and marketing tracking. Google/Meta identifiers are blank by default and cannot collect production data until configured.

Cookie and tracking controls

The consent banner offers reject non-essential, analytics only and accept all choices. Google consent signals default to denied. Meta tracking loads only after marketing consent.

Before production launch, Amcore should publish the final data-controller details, retention periods, CRM processors, hosting provider, lawful bases, data-subject rights, complaint route and complete cookie/storage inventory.

Lead forms

Project forms capture contact details and project information only when the visitor submits them. Marketing consent is separated from the service enquiry.

The prototype contains no live CRM endpoint. When one is configured, the privacy notice should name or describe the relevant processors and explain how enquiries are handled.
