COMMERCIAL ENERGY

Decarbonise comfort without disrupting operations.

For commercial buildings, the cheapest system on day one is rarely the best-performing system over its life. Amcore looks at use patterns, zoning, controls, maintenance access and electrical capacity before recommending equipment.

01

Survey & load profile

Occupancy, fabric, heat gains, operating hours, existing plant and electrical capacity.

02

System architecture

Split, multi-split, VRF/VRV, heat pump, solar, battery and controls selected as a coordinated system.

03

Phased delivery

Plan around business continuity, budgets and future expansion rather than forcing an all-or-nothing retrofit.

04

Maintain & optimise

PPM, refrigerant compliance, performance checks and ongoing tuning to protect the investment.
